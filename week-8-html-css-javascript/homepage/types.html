Personality types

Grunge
Late 1980s and early 1990s vibes, reflects a sense of authenticity, emphasizes individuality and rebellion against norms.
Princess
Hyper feminine and elegant visual style, inspired by fairy tales and historical royalty and shows that the fantasies can be brought to reality.
Nerdy/Corporate
Deeply engaged in academic spirits, intellectual pursuits, loves reading and passinate about a dedicated lifestyle.
Y2K
Retro-futuristic style, love exploring new trends and technologies but also loves nostalgic celebration of life.
Soft/Cute
Kawaii, cutesy and hyper feminine vibes, celebrates innocence, positivity and self-expression, doll like persona.
Fashionista
Passionate about fashions, keeps up with the latest trends, confident, carries an attitude of creativity and influential among others.
